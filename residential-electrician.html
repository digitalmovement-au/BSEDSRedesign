Data Points, Power Points and Safety Switches

We install and repair telephone, data and NBN points so every room has strong, reliable internet, whether you work from home or stream in the evenings.

Extra power and USB points mean fewer overloaded power boards and double adaptors, and we add safety switches (RCDs) to older homes that are missing protection.

  • Telephone, data and NBN points
  • Power points and USB outlets
  • Safety switches (RCDs) on every circuit
